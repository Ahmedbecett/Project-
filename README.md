## 🌟 Key Features / أبرز ميزات التطبيق

### 1. 🌍 Learn Multiple Languages
Seamlessly switch and learn multiple languages with native text-to-speech audio:
- 🇪🇸 Spanish (Español)
- 🇫🇷 French (Français)
- 🇩🇪 German (Deutsch)
- 🇮🇹 Italian (Italiano)
- 🇬🇧 English
- 🇸🇦 Arabic (العربية)
- 🇯🇵 Japanese (日本語)
- 🇧🇷 Portuguese (Português)

### 2. 🎯 Complete CEFR Path: A1 to C2
Structured curricula covering every step of language proficiency:
- **A1**: Beginner (Basic everyday expressions & introductions)
- **A2**: Elementary (Routine interactions & past narratives)
- **B1**: Intermediate (Independent travel, debate & opinions)
- **B2**: Upper Intermediate (Professional correspondence & fluency)
- **C1**: Advanced (Idiomatic mastery, rhetoric & subtle cultural nuances)
- **C2**: Mastery (Literary stylistics & native precision)

### 3. 🗣️ Real-Life Conversations (8 Situations)
Realistic, interactive dialogue roleplay with speech recognition & instant pronunciation scoring:
- ✈️ **Airport & Flight Boarding** (Travel)
- 🏨 **Hotel Check-In & Service**
- 🍽️ **Gourmet Dining & Restaurant Orders**
- 🛍️ **Boutique Shopping & Sizes**
- 💼 **Job Interviews & Career Discussion**
- 🎓 **University & Campus Life**
- 👋 **Meeting People & Networking**
- 🏠 **Daily Life & Health Consultations**

### 4. 🎙️ Smart Pronunciation Practice
- High-quality native **Text-To-Speech (TTS)** audio playback for every word and sentence.
- Microphone input with real-time phonetics and pronunciation accuracy percentage scoring (0–100%) and personalized coaching tips.

### 5. 🎯 Diagnostic Placement Test
- 12-question adaptive diagnostic exam to accurately estimate your starting CEFR level.

### 6. 🏆 Comprehensive Level Final Exams & Digital Certificates
- Multi-section timed examinations (Grammar, Vocabulary, Reading, Listening, Speaking).
- Passing candidates (70%+) receive a **Verifiable LinguaQuest Digital Certificate** with a unique credential ID and shareable export.

### 7. 🧠 Spaced Repetition Vocabulary & Flashcards
- Categorized cards with phonetic guides, parts of speech, and translated context examples.
- Filter by *Need Review*, *Mastered*, or search instantly.

### 8. 🎮 Gamification & Dashboard
- XP reward system and daily study goals.
- Study streak fire counter (🔥).
- Achievements and milestone badges.
- Weekly League Global Leaderboard.
- Light and Dark Mode toggle, custom speech speed controls (0.8x, 1.0x, 1.2x).
